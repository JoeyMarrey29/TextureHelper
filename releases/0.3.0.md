# Update v0.3.0 - Skin Workshop

- Browse the community Skin Workshop inside the editor and download skins as editable local drafts.
- Sign in with Steam to publish skins with a captured preview, public attribution and explicit sharing consent.
- Uploads undergo automated validation before publication. View pending submissions, failure reasons, cancel your uploads and remove failed entries.
- Moderator tools include submission cancellation, published-skin removal and a searchable ban table.
- Workshop cards show Steam names and profile pictures. Authentication and permissions are enforced by the service, not the desktop UI.
- Saved draft cards now show previews with edited textures and attachment choices. Click a card to open it and use Publish, Export, Install/Uninstall and Delete inside the draft inspector.
- Added Refresh models and Workshop support for editor-compatible melee weapons, including the katana.

Uploads allow 40 attempts per account per UTC day and 4,000 globally, with no lifetime cap. Failed attempts count; limits reset at midnight UTC. Service availability is subject to free infrastructure limits.

Downloads never install automatically. Workshop bans restrict the online Workshop, not the local editor. Existing drafts and installed skins are retained.
