Keep this texture's alpha channel?

        

        
Some models use alpha for shader masks rather than transparency, so their VTF can look blank in texture viewers. Removing alpha makes the installed VTF opaque, but can change reticle transparency or in-game shader effects.

        
Your original draft, preview and PNG/DDS exports will keep their pixels. This choice affects only installation.
